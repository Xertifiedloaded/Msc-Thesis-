# Comparison of Nonlinear and Regression Models for Chicken Growth Curves

A pre-data seminar proposal comparing nonlinear growth models and regression approaches for describing growth curves in **FUNAAB Alpha (dual-purpose)** and **Noiler chickens**.

## Research focus

This study proposes to compare candidate models for describing body-weight growth across age in two chicken genotypes—FUNAAB Alpha and Noiler—under a common management system.

### Objectives

- Fit nonlinear growth models (Gompertz, Logistic, von Bertalanffy, Richards and Brody) alongside linear and polynomial regression models.
- Estimate and compare growth parameters, including mature/asymptotic weight, maturation rate, and inflection-point age and weight where applicable.
- Compare model fit using R², RMSE, AIC, BIC and residual diagnostics.
- Evaluate sex and genotype-by-sex effects on growth trajectories.
- Compare growth characteristics between FUNAAB Alpha and Noiler chickens.
